RITU'S BIRTHDAY SURPRISE
=======================

OPEN IT
Double-click index.html in this folder. No installation, account, or build
step is required. The entrance code is 1711 (17 November).
The birthday is 17 November 1998. Her full name is Ritika; her nickname is Ritu.
The photos and interactions work offline. Spotify needs internet access.

PERSONALIZE IT
Open config.js in a text editor. Change the name, your sign-off, secretCode,
personal letter, captions, and wishes. Save and refresh index.html.
The headings, letter, wishes, buttons and messages use conversational English.
Only the user's exact personal lines, photo captions and song title retain
their original wording. The branding is "For my RJ". The entrance has only
the date riddle, with no separate code hint. The code remains 1711.
You can change "Your future husband" to your name.

PHOTOS
The eight Card photos are copied into photos/memories for the camera.
The nine usss photos are copied into photos/us for the couple gallery.
ThisDayLastYear.jpeg is in the couple gallery, matching the updated folders.
The originals in Downloads have not been changed.
Captions use the filenames with spaces added between words, preserving the
original spelling. config.js has separate memories and gallery collections.
Keep the photos folder together with the site when sharing or publishing.
Tap a gallery photo to view it larger. Use Previous/Next or the arrow keys
to browse, and Escape or the close button to return.

MUSIC
The music is the film's instrumental theme, "Love At First Sight" by Anand
Raj Anand, from the Rehnaa Hai Terre Dil Mein soundtrack. This replaces the
vocal title song; no vocals have been extracted or recordings downloaded.
Soundtrack: https://open.spotify.com/album/2BqTOf0JUe4yWKtrOA30V8
Direct track: https://open.spotify.com/track/7rWlLVZL4MI8oDADfrG4hy
Spotify's official player appears directly in the song section after you
unlock the surprise. Press Play there; no extra loader button is required.
The header's "our song" button takes you to the same player. Nothing autoplays.
Spotify is contacted after unlocking, not while the entrance is locked.
Playback depends on Spotify availability and may require a sign-in or offer
only a preview. The direct link remains available if the embed is blocked.
Switching tabs keeps the player in place. Relocking unloads it to stop playback;
unlocking again restores it. Returning via browser history restores it as well.
No copyrighted recording has been downloaded or bundled.
Optional local audioFile and the original synthesized melody remain available
when no Spotify player is configured.

GITHUB PAGES
Site: https://kapilbindal.github.io/for-my-rj/
Repository: https://github.com/kapilbindal/for-my-rj
Publishing source: the main branch, / (root).
To update the public site, update the corresponding files in this repository
and push to main. Keep index.html, styles.css, app.js and config.js together,
include the photos folder, and retain the repository's .nojekyll marker.

To share offline, zip this entire folder. The recipient must extract it before
opening index.html. Sending only index.html will not include the other files.

PRIVACY
The four-digit entrance is a playful reveal, NOT authentication or encryption.
Anyone who has the files or a public site link can inspect its code and photos.
Do not include intimate/private material or sensitive information. For actual
privacy, use access-controlled hosting; an unguessable link is not a password.
The site itself has no analytics, data collection, microphone, or camera access.
Loading the optional Spotify player contacts Spotify and is subject to its
privacy practices. Your photo files are not sent to Spotify.
The illustrated camera reveals supplied memories; it does not take photos.
The GitHub repository and published site are public. Only include photos and
personal details that you are comfortable making publicly accessible.

DESIGN
A standalone, original sky-blue romantic keepsake inspired by the interaction
ideas in the example you shared. No example-site source, photos, stickers,
personal letter, or copyrighted music has been copied.
